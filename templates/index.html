How it works

PhishGuard extracts 24 lexical and host-based features from a URL (length, use of IP addresses, suspicious keywords, link shorteners, risky TLDs, HTTPS usage, DNS resolution, and more) and feeds them into a trained Random Forest classifier to estimate the probability that the URL is a phishing attempt.
